## Origin story (Startitup, 12 Dec 2024)
- Before the café, the couple ran coffee picnics outdoors — friends called it the
  "mobile café".
- Family and acquaintances doubted that opening another small café in Vranov was a
  good idea.
- Neighbours asked whether they would serve beer. They don't.
- Quote on the name: „Dodnes nám to robí veľkú radosť, keď počujeme ľudí dvíhať
  telefón so slovami: No veď sme v Nashej."
- Quote: „Teraz je to krásne. Máme najlepších zákazníkov." (Ivona)
- Philosophy: guests should feel at home; quality, friendliness, a relaxed place to
  rest, meet or work. Farmers should be paid fairly, roasters should get the best
  out of the beans, baristas should prepare each cup with care.
- „Myslíme si, že zákazníkom dnes omnoho viac záleží a nie je im ľahostajné, čo
  pijú." — they believe guests increasingly prefer lighter, fruitier roasts.

## Owners — Ivona and Peter Jurko (Jurkovci)
**Peter Jurko**
- Worked in a restaurant during high school; first full-time job in a hotel;
  bartender in Vranov, where he took his first barista courses.
- Moved to Banská Bystrica and started working for GASTROVIA group (Kaffa coffee);
  by Dec 2024 seven years there, described as head of coffee and roaster for Kaffa.
- Startitup (Feb 2025) calls him one of Slovakia's top baristas. Quote used as the
  headline: „Káva nie je jadrová fyzika. Pohostinnosť máme v DNA."
- First "truly good" coffee: a sweet, honey-like Jamaican coffee at an SCA training.
  Today prefers African coffees (earlier Central American).
- Pictured on Kaffa's "About us" page (role not stated).
- **Slovakian AeroPress Championship 2026 — 1st place** (Banská Bystrica, 3 Oct 2026,
  listed as Kaffa). Earns the right to represent Slovakia at the world final in Mexico City,
  6 Dec 2026. Source: [Kávičkári, 4 Oct 2026](https://kavickari.sk/nulty-rocnik-aeropress-championship/).
- Slovak Roasting Masters 2025 (Košice): a search summary says he competed and placed 2nd;
  the organiser's page I opened did not list him. **Unverified.**
- Other competition results: the Startitup interview mentions them but they are behind a
  paywall. **Ask Peter.**
- Whether he still works for GASTROVIA in 2026: **unknown.**

**Ivona Jurková**
- Came to coffee through Peter; moved to Banská Bystrica to be with him and learned

## Coffee
- Main grinder: 100 % Arabica blend, darker roast (espresso, milk drinks).
- Second grinder: rotating, usually a lighter, fruitier roast.
- Main brand: Kaffa (GASTROVIA group, Považská Bystrica, roasting since 2018).
  In 2024 Kaffa opened its own roastery in Vranov, where Peter roasts.
- Instagram now says "pražiareň" — whether the café now sells its **own** roast
  under the Nasha name, and how it relates to Kaffa, is **unverified**. This decides
  how the "coffee we roast" section is written.
- In the past they also served The Barn (Germany) and BeBerry.
- Lever espresso machine. Brew methods: batch brew, V60, AeroPress; cold brew in
  summer. Beans sold in the shop.

## Drinks and food
- Seasonal: matcha variations, coffee lemonades, "superlattes" (beetroot, turmeric),
  chai latte.
- Desserts mainly from the nearby confectionery **Kejtlyn**; cakes from
  **Martin Devečka** (Liptovský Mikuláš) were popular. Reviews mention biscuits,
  cheesecake.
- Neighbouring bakery **Nandy** — guests bring its bread to eat with coffee.
- Breakfast boxes ("raňajkové škatule") are mentioned in a paywalled section.
  **Unverified.**
